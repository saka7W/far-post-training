What we work on

Every session is about getting better with the ball, moving well, and reading the game. Open to players from any team.

Ball skills

First touch, dribbling, and passing under pressure.

Attacking

Finishing, 1v1s, and creating space.

Defending

Positioning, pressing, and winning the ball back.

Game sense

Making quicker decisions in small-sided games.

Fitness and confidence

Speed, agility, and playing with more belief.
